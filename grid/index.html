Grid Playground

A grid that shows up to a number of columns, and drops columns on its own when they would get narrower than a min width. There are no breakpoints: it reacts to the space it's in, so the same grid works in a full page, a sidebar or a modal. Each cell is also a container, so its content can adapt to the cell's width. Drag the corner of the grid or move the sliders.
